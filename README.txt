Sumant — After Dark: The Quiet Hours

Complete static website, with your Temple Night scene, social links, and new night garden.

To preview: open a terminal inside this extracted folder, run python3 -m http.server 8000, and visit http://localhost:8000. Do not double-click index.html; JavaScript modules require a web server. Upload the folder contents to a static website host to publish.

Stay a while opens the garden. A guest silhouette slowly appears beside the host. You can pause the atmosphere; reduced-motion preferences are respected. The back button or Escape returns to the homepage.

Music: official Spotify players for I Think They Call This Love by Elliot James Reay and Co2 by Prateek Kuhad, with optional official YouTube players. Select a track, then press Play in its player. Spotify may provide a preview, with full-track access depending on its account/region rules. YouTube may restrict embedded playback; use Open on YouTube if needed. Changing track/provider or leaving the garden removes the prior player and stops playback. No copyrighted song audio is included, downloaded, or rehosted in this package.

Files: index.html (content), style.css (homepage), garden.css and garden.js (garden), main.js and templeNightRenderer.js (original scene), vendor/three.module.js (Three.js r149), assets/ (generated garden images).

Garden artwork was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Final generation/edit prompts are in ARTWORK-PROMPTS.txt.

Internet is required for streaming music and Google Fonts; the site includes system font fallbacks.
